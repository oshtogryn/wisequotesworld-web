## Visual concept — WQ011
Premium author-specific micro-film. Nietzsche remains the central visual identity through a dignified dark-bronze sculpture inspired by late-19th-century portraiture. The thought is visualized through pressure and survival: darkness, subtle cracks in the pedestal, then a controlled reveal into warmer light. This metaphor belongs to WQ011; do not mechanically reuse it for other authors.

## Shared production direction
- Vertical 9:16, photorealistic, prestigious editorial cinema.
- Immediate extreme/close three-quarter hook on Nietzsche’s bronze face and moustache; no black intro.
- Gallery starts nearly dark. Narrow warm light crosses the face; subtle cool rim and faint dust add depth.
- Slow pull-back/orbit reveals subtle cracks in the stone pedestal and tiny fragments below. Sculpture remains solid and imposing; no destruction.
- As the quote reaches its final idea, warmer light grows behind the sculpture and camera resolves into a strong low three-quarter angle.
- One stable quote card, then remove it completely; final stable author card.
- Deep calm native male narrator; restrained low cello/felt-piano atmosphere below voice; subtle rise at reveal, never trailer music.
- No fake historical footage, living impersonator, superhero treatment or motivational-ad cliché.
- No subtitles, captions, emoji, decorative symbols, modern objects, additional people, random readable text, generated logo, branding or watermark.
